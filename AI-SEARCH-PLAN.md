# Search and AI discovery plan

The site is designed for both standard search engines and AI-assisted answers (Google AI Overviews/AI Mode, Bing/Copilot, and other systems that find public webpages).

## Implemented
- Semantic, crawlable HTML with service-specific pages
- Clear Chinchilla and Miles coverage
- Business name and telephone in visible text
- LocalBusiness JSON-LD with accurate, publicly supported service descriptions
- Customer question-and-answer content on the homepage
- No blocking of standard crawlers in robots.txt

## Before launch
- Verify the precise domain purchased, then add canonical URLs and an XML sitemap.
- Confirm all business details, especially the telephone number and service areas.
- Obtain genuine team/work photos, accurate descriptive alt text, and evidence of completed work.
- Add a visible privacy notice and spam-protected enquiry form, only once a data-processing destination is chosen.
- Enable GitHub Pages and configure domain DNS; avoid changing email DNS records.
- Verify Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools; submit the sitemap.
- Keep Google Business Profile services and website text consistent.
- Link relevant service pages from the main menu and any authoritative local partner listings.

## Ongoing
- Publish practical before/after case studies about real Chinchilla projects, with customers' consent.
- Answer customer questions based on real job experience (e.g. mowing frequency, property access, large commercial sites).
- Request genuine customer reviews without incentives or selective review gating.
- Check real queries, indexed pages and AI citations where the platforms provide reports.
- Avoid mass-produced suburb pages, inflated reviews or unsubstantiated claims.

Google does not require an llms.txt file or special AI schema markup for AI features. Avoid claiming any file guarantees LLM citations.
