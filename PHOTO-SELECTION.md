## Publication checklist
- Confirm the image depicts Hairy's work (and avoid implying a scene is an after photo unless verified).
- Exclude fence construction photos; incidental boundary fencing in garden scenes is fine.
- No identifiable people, children, house numbers, number plates or sensitive client details without appropriate permission.
- Convert to optimised WebP/AVIF with responsive variants; preserve original locally rather than putting full-size originals online.
- Write descriptive, accurate alt text.
- Do not publish uploaded files until they are staged through a supported binary asset workflow and approved.
